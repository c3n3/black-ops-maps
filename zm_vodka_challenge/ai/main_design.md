# Vodka Challenge: Design Plan

**Map type:** Black Ops 3 Custom Zombies
**Layout:** Linear, one straight line from start to finish
**Atmosphere:** Concrete, bright lights
**Weapons:** Standard BO3 weapons. The Wunderwaffe is the wonder weapon.
**Spawn logic:** Standard zombie spawn logic from Void Challenge, plus pre-endgame spawns and power-ups from zm_parkour_nothing0. No endgame logic from zm_parkour_nothing0.

## Door Types
- **Money doors:** Normal doors. Price starts at $1,000 and increases by $1,000 per door.
- **Vodka doors:** The vodka machine is the door. Players must drink the required number of drinks to pass. Any player can drink toward the total.

## 1. Start Room (Vodka Door: 3 × N drinks)
- Four separate cells, one per spawn point. Each player walks around inside their own cell.
- Each cell has a vodka machine, copied from the other map.
- All four cells share a common door, which is a vodka door.
- The door opens once the group reaches 3 × N drinks, and the first round begins.

## 2. Drop Chute
- The four cells lead into a single chamber below them.
- Players drop through a chute. The fall deals damage but cannot kill.
- Players land with a red screen effect.

## 3. Common Room
- Two dark hallways on either side, each with zombie spawns.
- No boards on the door. A player clip prevents running through it.

## 4. First Chamber (M14 Room, Money Door: $1,000)
- Square room, large enough to dodge attacks but too small to train zombies.
- Contains the **M14**.
- A hallway through the center of the far wall leads to the next door.
- A vodka machine sits in a cubby to the right of the door.
- **Respawn point:** Once this door opens, dead players respawn here. Before that, they respawn in the start room.

## 5. Repeating Chamber Pattern (Chambers 2–10, Money Doors)
Each chamber follows the same structure:
- A square room with a few items, including a **perk machine** (random, with **Speed Cola in 4th position** and **Juggernog in 10th position** in the chain).
- A hallway through the far wall leads to the next door.
- A vodka machine sits in a cubby to the right of the door.
- Chambers cascade downward. Walkways between them are slanted down.
- Between chambers, alternate between a **wall gun** (increasing in value) and a **box location**.
- Every chamber gets proper info volumes.
- **Door costs:** Money, continuing the $1,000 + $1,000-per-door scale.

## 6. Mid-Map Hub (After Chamber 10, Vodka Door: 2 × N drinks)
- Large concrete room, big enough for training zombies.
- **Eight dark spawn rooms**, four on each side. These are the zombie spawns. They are not player doors.
- One exit door at the back wall, which is the vodka door.
- Starting box is on the back wall.
- Perk machine is at the back wall.

## 7. Chambers 11–20 (Money Doors)
- Same repeating pattern as chambers 2–10.
- Door costs continue scaling by $1,000 per door.

## 8. Final Room (Vodka Door: 1 × N drinks)
- Large room, matching the mid-map hub's scale.
- Dog spawn in the center.
- Pack-a-Punch at the far end.
- Wunderfizz / box location.
- **Dingo** mounted on the end wall.
