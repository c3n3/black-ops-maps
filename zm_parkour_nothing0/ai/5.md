# Lighthouse

- There is a light way off in the distance. Make a lighthouse there using simple brushes.
- Inside the lighthouse is a switch that, when pulled, causes a nuke to go off.
- It is ok if there is no way for a player to get there as of right now.

# Q/A

- Q: What should pulling the switch do? Instant nuke, or drop a nuke powerup?
  - A: Instant nuke
- Q: How often can the switch be used?
  - A: Once per game
- Q: What should happen to the existing distant light and white octagon disc?
  - A: Lamp at the light (build the tower so its lantern room surrounds the existing lights, remove the white disc)
- Q: How tall should the lighthouse be, and what does it stand on?
  - A: From platform level (base at z 0, about 500 tall to the lamp, on a small island)
