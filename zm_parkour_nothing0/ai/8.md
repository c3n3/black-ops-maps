# Endgame exit

- During the endgame, at the spawn, an arrow appears to the side of the spawn place.
- The arrow points down and away, telling the players to jump off.
- There is a platform at the bottom of the jump with a 500 dollar buyable ending.
- Players take no fall damage on this platform, and zombies cannot make it there.
- The buyable ending platform does not exist until the endgame.

# Q/A

- Q: What should buying the $500 ending do?
  - A: End the game for all (one player buys it, normal game-over screen)
- Q: When can the ending be bought?
  - A: Endgame only
- Q: Which side of spawn, and how far down is the platform?
  - A: West, about 1000 down (512x512 platform, well above the sky-floor down zone at -2500)
- Q: What should the arrow look like?
  - A: Glowing green brushes (hidden until the endgame, tilted down and away from spawn)
