# Black Ops 3 Zombies Map Design Plan

## Overview
Extending the map up to the lighthouse by adding "The Void" zone and a lighthouse approach path.

---

## The Void Zone

### Entrance
- Two blood-written signs: left reads "V", right reads "void"
- Debris blocks the entrance
- Each sign has a stand beneath it with a bowl/cauldron fire for illumination

### Layout & Platforms
- Platforms slightly larger than current octagon platforms
- Platforms positioned slightly closer together than normal zones (easier jumping)
- Zone is **triple the size** of current zones in both horizontal and vertical directions
- **No candles or posts on platforms** — completely dark except for:
  - Central light pole (faulty, continuously flickering)
  - Interactable campfires on approximately half the platforms

### Central Light Pole
- Located in center of void
- Faulty, continuously flickering light
- Electrical spark effects on wires at base
- Activates via switch to turn on

### Campfires
- Placed on approximately half of void platforms
- Interactable: players can light them
- Stone ring around each campfire for aesthetic
- Lit campfire illuminates just enough to cover that platform
- **Damage mechanic:** Standing on lit campfire causes burn damage to player

### Enemies & Spawning
- **Witches** spawn occasionally (mixed into horde, not large quantities) when player is in void
- **One Warden** spawns in center next to light pole when player opens door to void
- Dog spawn locations needed across all zones for end game spawning
- Warden spawn locations needed for end game spawning

---

## Lighthouse Approach

### Normal Zone Continuation
- Standard zone pattern continues from void to lighthouse
- Exits void and transitions to normal platforms

### Final Walkway (400 units before lighthouse)
- Small narrow walkway players must walk straight across
- Jump required between:
  - Normal platforms → walkway
  - Walkway → lighthouse platform

### Walkway Trap
- **Cost:** $5,000
- **Effect:** Lights walkway on fire and kills all zombies on it
- **Duration:** 2 minutes
- **Delay:** 1 minute cooldown before reuse

---

## Respawn Mechanics

### Invisible Safety Platforms (3 total)
- Located on bottom of map (no player collision, invisible)
- Positions: under spawn, under lighthouse, in center
- Each has bright neon green circle underneath
- Neon lighting on circles for visibility

### Green Circle Mechanic
- **Action:** Player shoots green circle
- **Effect:** Triggers teleportation animation (matching teleporters/giant)
- **Destination:**
  - Pre-game: Teleports to spawn
  - In-game: Teleports to lighthouse

### Instructional Sign
- Placed at map start
- Text: "shoot the green circles for 'safety'."

# Q/A

- Q: The mod tools only ship zombie and dog AI; there's no witch or warden. How should Witches and the Warden be made?
  - A: Defer them
- Q: Where does everything go in the chain north? (z9 ends at y 11968, the lighthouse island starts at y 24176)
  - A: z9 → Void → normal zones → walkway → lighthouse
- Q: Do the new normal zones between the Void and the lighthouse follow the full z1–z9 pattern?
  - A: Full pattern (door with escalating cost, perk or machine, box spot or wall gun)
- Q: The .map and the FX live outside the usermaps repo. Should the commits track them?
  - A: Copy into repo (snapshot the .map and .efx files into zm_parkour_nothing0/source/ at each stage)
- Q: "Triple the size in both horizontal and vertical directions": wider and longer, or also 3x the height range?
  - A: 3x wide & long (top-down), platforms at the same height as now
- Q: How many normal zones between the Void and the walkway?
  - A: 6 zones
- Q: The full pattern needs perks, but all are used. Proposal: repeat Jugg, Quick Revive, Speed, Double Tap, Stamin-Up, Mule Kick; guns stronger going north M8A1 → SCAR-H → MK48; box locations 5–7; door costs keep escalating +750 per zone (Void door 7500)
  - A: Use proposal
- Q: How should the blood-written "V" and "void" signs be made?
  - A: Letter brushes (thin brushes painted with a blood decal material on a dark board, on a post with a fire bowl)
- Q: Central light pole: where's its switch, what does it cost, what does "turn on" look like?
  - A: Switch at the pole base, free. Off until pulled, then on and flickering forever. Sparks at the base wires always.
- Q: Campfires: cost to light, and burn damage?
  - A: Free to light, stays lit; standing in the fire deals about 10 damage every 0.5 s
- Q: Walkway trap: where's the $5,000 switch, and does the fire hurt players?
  - A: On the edge of the lighthouse island so that it is obvious, and the fire does hurt players too
- Q: What should the endgame do with the dog spawn locations?
  - A: Mix dogs in (after the endgame starts, dogs spawn occasionally from dog locations in every zone alongside zombies)
- Q: How should the safety platforms work?
  - A: The green circles should be just below the normal paths, only visible from below. Falling / downed players can shoot the circles in time to save themselves from death.
- Q: "Pre-game → spawn / in-game → lighthouse": what's the switch-over point?
  - A: Lighthouse reached (before any player has reached the lighthouse island → spawn; after → lighthouse)
- Q: How should the start sign text be shown?
  - A: Board + look-at text
- Q: What happens to a player who reaches the bottom?
  - A: Allow downed players to still shoot the circle, but make sure there is a decent falling period (as current) that allows non-downed players to shoot it. Make sure even PhD Flopper does not matter: if the player hits the bottom of the sky they are downed and crawl around.
- Q: How big should each circle be?
  - A: Medium (~500 wide)
- Q: When a downed player at the bottom shoots a circle and teleports, revived or still downed?
  - A: Still downed
