# More zones

- The map has a grid of floating octagons and two info zones. Extend the number of zones by 4x.
- Each zone needs a debris door just like the current one.
- Each zone needs a new perk.
- Each zone needs either a spot for the box, or a wall gun.
- Spooky prefabs are used for the octagons with perks / guns / boxes on them.

# Q/A

- Q: "Extend the number of zones by 4x": 8 total (6 new), or 8 new (10 total)?
  - A: 8 new, 10 total. Give Jugg towards the end. Also zones are meant to overlap by 1, so the previous zone still spawns zombies.
- Q: How should the new zones be laid out? Linear chain north, or branching?
  - A: Linear chain north
- Q: start_zone's volume covers almost all of z1, and debris0 has an empty script_flag with no add_adjacent_zone in the GSC, so z1 never activates as its own zone. Fix both?
  - A: Yes, fix both (shrink start_zone's volume to the start area, give every debris door a script_flag, add add_adjacent_zone calls)
- Q: Which zones get a box spot and which get a wall gun?
  - A: Alternate (even zones z2/z4/z6/z8 get a box location, odd zones z3/z5/z7/z9 get a wall gun)
- Q: "Zones overlap by 1": the zone manager already keeps the previous zone spawning through adjacency. What overlap do you want in the volumes themselves?
  - A: Adjacency only
- Q: There are only 6 unused perk machines but 8 new zones. What goes in the other 2 zones?
  - A: PaP + GobbleGum
- Q: How much should each new debris door cost?
  - A: Escalate by 750 per zone
- Q: The odd zones get wall guns. Which guns?
  - A: Stronger as you go, all BO2 guns
