# Endgame zombie recall

- During the endgame, any zombies that are a zone behind the player are immediately despawned and then respawned in z0.
- When done, package the map into a zip that can be sent to a friend, with instructions.

# Q/A

- Q: By "z0", do you mean the spawn zone (start_zone)?
  - A: Yes, start_zone (spawn)
- Q: During the endgame players head from the lighthouse back toward spawn. Which zombies count as "a zone behind"?
  - A: Behind every player
- Q: Should dogs get the same treatment (despawn and respawn at z0)?
  - A: Zombies only
