# Vodka Perk Plan

## Perk Name
Vodka

- Add to spawn island next to power

## Machine Design
- Use the DevRaw template (whitewashed)
- Text: "Vodka" in white
- Image: Bottle of vodka

## Cost
- Negative 1000 dollars
- Players gain 1000 dollars when purchasing

## Effect
- Drunken visual effect while player has the perk

## Persistence
- Perk stays with player even when they go down

## Stacking
- Players can buy multiple times
- Each purchase increases drunken visual effect intensity:
  - **1 purchase:** Slight effect, easily playable but noticeable
  - **2-4 purchases:** Gradually increasing intensity
  - **5 purchases:** Maxes out visual effect, very difficult to play

## Help Text
- Nikolai quote about vodka from Black Ops Zombies
